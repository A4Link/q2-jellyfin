# Changelog

All notable changes for users of q2-jellyfin. Format: [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased]

### Security

- **A server can no longer fill the card by sending more than a song's size:** a download of a song whose size is known now stops at twice that size and 8 MiB, also when the server's Content-Length says more, and the song fails. Before, a long Content-Length was taken as it was, so a hostile server could write past Space to Keep Free until the card was full. This covers songs streamed on Q2 Pod before 1.7.3 too.
- **A hostile server can no longer use up the Q2's memory with one answer:** an answer whose lists hold more than 50,000 values in all is refused with *Answer too large for the Q2*, as one over 8 MB is: 8 MB of tiny values such as `0` or `null` took over 1 GB to read. A real page of 500 songs holds about 500 to 1,500. An answer's headers are capped at 64 KB (they were allowed 10 MB); bigger ones fail as a server that can't be reached does. Of an item's image tags only its cover's is read, so any others in an answer are skipped instead of kept in memory.
- **A very long song title, artist or album no longer crashes the app at Play** under Q2 Pod's `urls`: each is cut to 1,000 bytes in the play queue, so the song pressed always fits in it. Before, a field over 256 KB from the server made every Play of that song exit the app, and with it a stream or sync under way.
- **Your sign-in is no longer written into the stream queue:** with Q2 Pod's `urls`, the queue that Q2 Pod's own player plays held each song's and cover's server address with your access token in it, and that player doesn't check the server's certificate, so someone on the same Wi-Fi posing as your server could take the token. The queue now points at the app, on the Q2's loopback (127.0.0.1:2317, which nothing on the network can reach), and the app fetches each song and cover over its own verified HTTPS connection, the token in a header; a song it can't fetch because the server can't be reached shows in Settings as Not connected, as a list does. On a Q2 Pod with `now` (1.8.1 or later), Jellyfin now stays running while a queue that streams plays (a queue of only synced songs doesn't keep it running), with Report Plays to Server on or off, and closes 30 minutes after it was paused or stopped, as before; on an older Q2 Pod it closes 30 minutes after the last song or cover it streamed, so a queue whose synced songs play longer than that stops at the next streamed song. A queue stopped that way, paused that long, or resumed after Jellyfin was closed plays on only once Jellyfin is opened again (README, Known limitations). If you streamed this way on a network you don't trust, revoke the Q2 under Dashboard → Devices, then sign in again; a Q2 signed in with an API key: delete the key under Dashboard → API Keys. A queue you started before updating still holds the token until you play something else or switch the Q2 off.

### Changed

- **The finished sync's summary says when Sync changed during it:** a song, album, playlist or Entire Library added to or removed from Sync while a sync ran or was paused waits for the next sync. *Check Again* on the summary, or on a stopped sync's page, now says *Sync changed since it started*.
- **Choose Playlists on the Sync page says *Included via Entire Library*** with Entire Library in Sync, as the playlists on its page do, instead of *0 playlists in Sync*.
- **A Sync check that fails offers *Check Again*,** between the message and *Details*, as a stopped sync's page does. Before, the page had no row to try again.
- **A Sync check that runs past 20 seconds says *A big library takes minutes*** under *Checking server…*.
- **A first sync of a big library, or a Sync Quality change, writes less to the card:** the sync records its downloads on the card every 20 songs or 1 in 200 of the songs on it, whichever is more, and still at least every 3 minutes. Each record rewrites the whole list of songs on the card, so with 50,000 songs a first sync writes it a half to a sixth as much as before (about 20 GB), less the faster songs arrive. A power cut part-way can fetch again up to that many songs (250 with 50,000 on the card), or those of the last 3 minutes. A Sync Quality change that gives songs a new format (FLAC to Opus) is recorded the same way, no longer once a song: with 50,000 FLAC songs that wrote about 800 GB to the card and took a second or more a song. A song's old file is deleted once the record names its new one; a stop before then leaves it listed, and the next sync deletes it. Songs taken out of Sync go the same way, up to 1,000 at a time: a Pause, a quit or the card pulled part-way leaves the rest listed on the card, and the next sync deletes them first. The Sync page counts these files (*Delete N files an earlier sync left*) and lists show *Sync Now*, so Start Sync is offered for them when nothing else changed; the sync then says *N files deleted*. Files already gone from the card (a sync that stopped on an error deleted them after its last record) leave that count when the Sync page checks, and are not counted as deleted again. A stop while the files are deleted leaves Last synced as it was. A batch of renamed songs is recorded once, before the files move, not again after. A sync's end no longer rewrites the list several times over with nothing new in it, and each rewrite is written a part at a time, with no copy of each song: it was built whole in memory first, which took about 27 MB more with 50,000 songs. Reading the list is a part at a time too: it read the whole file before decoding it, about 14 MB more with 50,000 songs. The list read keeps one copy of each song's id and of the quality and item names songs share, about 3.7 MB less with 50,000 songs.
- **New connections to the server are quicker to set up:** a connection opened after a minute's pause, or alongside others, resumes the earlier secure session instead of a full handshake, which is slow on the Q2. Browsing after a pause, seeking in a streamed song and loading covers spend less time waiting. The server's certificate is still checked by name and date. With a server that speaks only TLS 1.2, each connection's keys also cross the network inside a session ticket sealed with the server's ticket key, so traffic recorded then, including the password sent at sign-in, can be read if that key ever leaks; TLS 1.3 servers are not affected.
- **A sync's album covers use little memory, whatever their size:** cover.jpg is written to the card as it arrives, instead of read whole first, and copied the same way into an album's new folder when a tag change renames it. A server that can't resize images sends the original, which can be many megabytes. A cover over 16 MiB is still not saved, and one whose size the server gives as over 16 MiB is not fetched. A cover that fails is now only written to the log, not kept in a list no page shows. An album folder left with only its cover and a cover.jpg.tmp (from a power cut during a cover download) is now removed with its last song.
- **A sync rewrites only the playlist files that changed:** a playlist whose song list is the same as the file on the card is no longer written again at the end of each sync, which saves time and card wear with many playlists in Sync.
- **Play asks the server for fewer lyrics on Q2 Pod 1.7.3 and later:** a Play fetches the lyrics of the song started and the 19 after it, not of every song in the queue (up to 500, one after another). A song further on has its lyrics once a later Play reaches it. Past 100 songs' lyrics kept between Plays, only the oldest are dropped, not all of them, so a big queue no longer fetches them all again at every Play. The app keeps at most 64 KB of each song's lyrics, the most the Q2 shows.
- **Quality and Storage no longer shows Playback Cache on Q2 Pod 1.7.3 and later:** the Q2 streams songs itself there and nothing is added to the cache. On any Q2 Pod, **Clear Playback Cache** shows only while the cache holds songs; on 1.7.3 and later that is songs cached under older firmware, which a sync still takes instead of downloading them again.
- **Big libraries (tens of thousands of songs) use less memory during a sync**, and pages no longer wait several seconds while the list of synced songs is read: the Sync page no longer reads that list again after each save of a running sync, a page, a press or a streamed song waits for it only when it needs the list itself, and the Q2's answer to a library update reads it only when there is a new playlist to record. Starting a sync no longer copies the list of songs to fetch, or looks for each in an empty playback cache; fetched songs no longer keep the server's file path in memory, and a list's later pages no longer build the Sync Now row they don't show. A finished sync no longer keeps its list of songs in memory (one that ended with failed songs keeps it for *Retry Failed* until the next sync is worked out), working out the Sync page no longer copies the list of songs to fetch, and sending play reports looks through the synced songs only when something new was played, so Report Plays and Sign Out no longer wait for it. Working out Entire Library no longer keeps the last check's list of songs in memory while it lists them again, or after Entire Library is switched off; Sync Entire Library keeps its album and playlist counts during a check, while album rows show *On Q2* again only once a check succeeds, as after an app start. A song or playlist page that fails is asked for once more before the check fails, and a check no longer stops after 10 minutes with *Can't reach* the server: only the lyrics it adds afterwards keep that limit.
- **The Sync page is ready sooner with many single songs, albums or playlists in Sync:** songs added one by one are looked up together, up to 100 in one request, instead of one request each, and so are albums and playlists. An item the server leaves out of that answer is still looked up on its own. With Entire Library, each server playlist is no longer looked up on its own: the playlist list already gives its name. Working out the Sync page no longer downloads each song's play count and other play data, which it doesn't use, for Entire Library or for the songs, playlists and albums it looks up, nor for the album list; an album's or playlist's page still does, for the heart in Now Playing.

### Fixed

- **The sign-in is now encrypted on the Q2:** its key was to be kept in a folder the Q2 doesn't have, so the token in `.jellyfin/credentials` was stored on the card unencrypted. The key is now kept in the Q2's own storage, and the next start encrypts the sign-in already there, writing over the unencrypted copy; no new sign-in is needed on that Q2. If the Q2's own storage can't take the key (it is full or read-only), the sign-in stays unencrypted until it can, and `.jellyfin/log.txt` says so. In another Q2 the card's sign-in can't be read, so Jellyfin asks you to sign in again. If a card was lost, lent or copied before this version (a backup, or a move to a new card), revoke the Q2 under **Dashboard → Devices** and sign in again: the update encrypts the same token, and older unencrypted copies may remain in the card's free space or in those copies.
- **The clock is set only from a server reached over `https://`:** on a Q2 whose clock is unset, an `http://` address in the card's `.jellyfin/server` file no longer sets the time, as its answer can't be checked. A server's time is also never taken from before the date of the app's certificate list (was 2025), so a certificate that expired before then can't set the clock back to when it was valid.
- **The Q2 no longer freezes for a moment when Jellyfin starts:** the app is copied from the card in the background, so the screen and buttons answer while it starts.
- **A server that stops showing the account its music library no longer empties the Q2:** the Sync preview says *No music library on the server for this account* and removes nothing, where it read as an empty library and planned every synced song for removal. Sync Favourites, and a sync restarting after a Hold, Resume or quality change, no longer start by themselves when the sync would remove songs: they wait for *Start Sync*, and their message no longer says the sync is starting; Sync Favourites in Sync reads *In Sync · press to check for new hearts*. Sync Favourites also resumes Favourites held in **Sync Jobs**, which added no new hearts. A preview that removes more than half the synced songs says *Removes most of the synced songs*.
- **An item in Sync that was deleted on the server no longer stops every sync:** an album, artist, playlist or song the server no longer has failed every Sync preview with *Not found on the server*, without saying which item, so nothing could sync until it was found and taken out. The preview now names it (*Abbey Road · no longer on the server*; past five such items, the rest are counted in one row) and lists its songs on the Q2 under Removed, unless something else in Sync still has them. They go only when you press **Start Sync**, which also takes the item out of Sync; until then **Sync Jobs** shows it as *No longer on the server*, with nothing to hold, and **On This Q2** shows it the same, with no page to open. The server answers the same when the account can no longer see the music library, and refuses its lists (Entire Library, Favourites, an artist) where the app said *Server refused this Q2. Sign in again*, so the preview then asks for the libraries again: with none left, it says *No music library on the server for this account* and removes nothing.
- **On This Q2: a press on Entire Library no longer takes it out of Sync.** The row is now titled Entire Library and does nothing when pressed, as the songs there do; switch it off on the Sync page. With nothing in Sync, the page now says *Open a playlist, album or artist and press Add to Sync*.
- **A server that fails every song no longer slows a sync:** after a song fails, the next one is tried once, without waiting, so a sync where every song fails (the server's music drive unmounted, its transcoder failing) gets through quickly instead of taking about 10 seconds a song with the Q2 kept out of standby. The sync still runs to its end: the songs that arrive are synced and the playlists written, and removals follow the usual rule. A song the server says isn't there is tried once, not three times. Failed songs are listed under *Failed Songs* on the Sync page also when a sync stops or pauses, and *Retry Failed* there resumes a paused sync and says *Sync started again*. While a sync runs, the song count says how many failed so far. *Failed Songs* lists the first 500 failed songs, and how many more failed. The Sync row in Settings says *Sync stopped* when a sync stopped.
- **One song that always fails no longer stops every removal:** a sync used to remove nothing (songs or playlists taken out of Sync, or gone from the server) whenever any song failed, so under Entire Library, where a song can't be taken out of Sync, one bad file on the server kept everything else on the Q2 for good. A single song failing no longer holds the removals back, nor do any under Entire Library. They still wait when the failures look server-wide (a song lost the connection) or, outside Entire Library, when several songs failed and fewer arrived, as when an album is swapped for one that doesn't come; the done page then says so: *N songs not removed: songs failed to sync*, with *Retry in Failed Songs* after a server-wide failure, and *Retry in Failed Songs, or take them out of Sync* after several songs' own failures (taking songs out of Sync is only for songs that failed on their own). Paused short of space for that reason, the Sync page says *Songs failed, so nothing was removed* and, after a server-wide failure, *Resume to try them again*; after several songs' own failures, *Resume, or take them out of Sync*. The pause at Space to Keep Free now reads *Paused: the card is nearly full*, as Settings says *Paused*, instead of *Stopped*, and the preview (*It pauses with 512 MB left*) and Quality and Storage (*A sync pauses before the card has less*) call it a pause too. Its *<free> free · keeping <setting> free* row says underneath how much the next song needs (*The next song needs 45 MB*), since that is why it paused while more than the setting is still free.
- **Pausing or quitting while a sync removes songs stops at once:** it no longer finishes deleting up to 1,000 songs first, past the 3 seconds the app gets when USB storage takes the card. The files it hadn't deleted yet stay listed on the card, and the next sync deletes them first; before, they could be left on the card unrecorded for good.
- **A Sync Quality change that makes songs smaller gets past a nearly full card:** the sync replaces songs before it adds new ones, so the old, bigger files go first. It added first, and stopped at the first new song every time, though the change would have made room. A replace that makes its song smaller goes ahead when it fits, and can take the card under Space to Keep Free by that one song until the next record deletes its old file.
- **A song whose Opus transcode the server stopped part-way is synced again**: when Jellyfin's encoder failed mid-song, or the connection was closed, the cut file was kept as the whole song and never fetched again until its quality or tags changed. A transcode is now kept only when it ends where an Ogg stream ends; otherwise the song fails, and the next sync brings it again. The Playback Cache, which a sync takes songs from, keeps no cut song either.
- **A song that changed on the server while its download was paused is no longer saved as two versions joined:** when the server's file changed size (a retag or a re-rip) and the next request for it failed, the part already on the card was continued with the new version's rest, and the song played a glitch partway through. The sync now starts such a song again from the beginning. A song the server refuses no longer leaves a small `.part.key` file and its album folder on the card, and a transcoded song that fails part-way no longer leaves its partial file, which a sync never continues.
- **A card that turns read-only or fails during a sync says so:** the sync stops with *The card is read-only. Check it in a computer* (or *Couldn't write to the card. Check it in a computer*), instead of pausing with *Can't reach* the server. A stream that can't write to the card says the same.
- **The playback cache keeps Space to Keep Free:** on Q2 Pod before 1.7.3, a played song is kept in the cache only if the card still has Space to Keep Free with it there; otherwise it is deleted, as with the cache off. A 2 to 8 GB cache could fill the card below that setting.
- **A playlist added back to Sync never replaces one you made of that name:** taken out of Sync, a playlist is no longer the app's, so after you delete the Q2's copy and make your own of the same name, adding the server's back to Sync no longer deletes yours; it arrives as *<name> (Jellyfin)* instead. So does a playlist you add back while the Q2 still has its earlier copy: delete that copy in **Library → Playlists** first. If the Q2 already has that *(Jellyfin)* name too, the Sync page says *Couldn't import <name>: the Q2 has a playlist with that name. Delete it in Library → Playlists, then Check Again* (with several, it names the first and counts the rest), and log.txt says so too. Until it is deleted, each preview offers a sync that tries again; once it is deleted, that sync adds it.
- **Jellyfin no longer stays open up to 2 hours after a sync**: it closes itself 30 minutes after the sync and your last press, as at any other time, even while the Q2 has yet to update its library (the Q2 waits while music plays). Smart Wi-Fi can then turn the radio off, unless a queue played from Jellyfin is the Q2's queue: Q2 Pod keeps Wi-Fi on for it, playing, paused or stopped. The Q2 updates its library anyway, and Jellyfin, when next opened, reads which playlists the Q2 imported. If the Q2 is switched off before Jellyfin has read the Q2's answer, Jellyfin asks again when next opened, so the Q2 updates its library again (Back cancels it). If the Q2 had already updated it, a playlist Jellyfin had added can come a second time as "<name> (Jellyfin)": a playlist you made is never replaced. Jellyfin also asks when next opened if it was stopped, or the Q2 switched off, in the seconds after "Sync complete", before it had asked the Q2.
## [0.8.0] - 2026-11-16

> **This app is 100% vibecoded.** It works, and every change was reviewed and tested, but the quality of its code cannot be assured. Use it at your own risk.

### Changed

- **Songs already in a lossy format are no longer re-encoded** under an Opus Sync or Streaming Quality: MP3, AAC, Ogg Vorbis, Opus, WMA and AC-3 files come as the server's file, untouched, since encoding them again only loses more. Lossless songs are transcoded as before. **Settings → Quality and Storage → Always Re-encode** (off by default) re-encodes lossy songs too, for smaller files; the row says underneath what its setting means. After updating, the next sync brings lossy songs synced at Opus again as their originals, unless Always Re-encode is turned on first. An account without the server's download permission needs Always Re-encode on to sync them: its sync now says so when it stops (README, Known limitations).
- **A synced list starts sooner while a big sync runs or is paused**: checking whether the sync is about to move or remove each of its songs is one lookup per song, not a walk through the whole sync (after a change of Sync Quality, every song on the card).
- **Removing many songs is quicker and wears the card less**: a sync records its removals on the card every 1,000 songs instead of every 100, as it does renames. A power cut part-way can now leave up to 1,000 removed songs' files on the card unrecorded (was 100); a song is still never recorded as on the card once its file is gone.
- **Start Sync answers sooner with a full Playback Cache**: it reads the cache folder once to find the songs the sync can take from it, instead of looking each planned song up in it.
- **Added and Removed on the Sync page scroll faster on a big sync**: each reply makes only the rows it shows, not one for every song in the sync.
- **The Sync page checks the server at most every 5 minutes** (was every minute), so opening it again soon after is quick, also with Entire Library in Sync; it no longer reads the sync records on the card twice either. An album added or a playlist changed on the server shows there up to 5 minutes later: *Check Again* looks at once, and changing what is in Sync or *Sync Favourites* does too.
- **Search asks the server less:** Search Artists asks only for artists, and Search Albums only for albums and songs, once for all its rows instead of again for the rest of them. On slow Wi-Fi the results show sooner.
- **Add to Sync answers at once**, without asking the server about every artist and playlist already in Sync. It still says *Included via* an artist, playlist or Favourites in Sync when Jellyfin has their songs to hand (since the Sync page or that item's page was opened); otherwise, as after a restart, the album or song is added on its own (*Added to Sync*): it is still synced once, and stays in Sync if that artist or playlist is removed.
- **Lists open sooner after Jellyfin starts:** the first list no longer waits while every top-level folder of the music libraries is fetched; that is now asked only when a playlist or song first needs it, once. On a big library over weak Wi-Fi the first list could time out again and again.
- **The Sync page is ready sooner with an artist in Sync:** each of the artist's albums is one request to the server, not two, so it takes half the time it did (an artist with many albums, such as "Various Artists" in a big library, could take long enough to time out).

### Fixed

- **Sync status follows the lossy rule:** under an Opus Sync Quality, an MP3 or AAC album synced as it is shows *On Q2* after a restart, instead of *Waiting to sync* and a *Sync Now* that finds nothing to do, and lossy songs at Opus that the next sync brings again as their originals show *Waiting to sync*. Always Re-encode turned on or off under Original no longer restarts a sync under way. A sync stopped because the account can't download gives the way round in a row of its own: *Turn on Always Re-encode in Quality and Storage*, or under Original *Or choose an Opus Sync Quality and Always Re-encode*.
- **A server that ignores where a list should continue** (or a proxy that caches its answers) no longer makes lists load until they time out: when a page doesn't start where it was asked to, the list says *The library changed while it was read. Try again* at once, instead of every list waiting and a Sync preview running out of memory.
- **A `Jellyfin sign-in.txt` over 4 KB is wiped before it is deleted**, as a smaller one is: it was deleted as too big to be a sign-in file with its bytes, its password among them, still on the card. The wipe writes the zeros a piece at a time, so a big file put there by mistake doesn't take its size in RAM.
- **The no-Wi-Fi hint names System settings:** without Wi-Fi, Jellyfin's main page says *Turn on Wi-Fi in System settings* (the Q2's own), not *Settings*, which read as Jellyfin's Settings row just below it.
- **Report Plays to Server: Off says so underneath:** *Plays aren't sent to your server*, instead of that your server counts the songs you play.
- **List covers stay within a limit in the Q2's memory:** the covers kept for lists are trimmed, oldest first, after every list that fetched one, to at most 1500 covers and under 8 MB, so a server that sends whole images instead of small ones no longer keeps filling the room streaming and the Q2's own pages need. A cover over 1 MB is no longer shown cut off: the row has no cover, and it isn't asked for again for 10 minutes.
- **A song that couldn't be streamed says why**, as the Sync page does: *Couldn't stream <song>: Can't reach <server>*, *The card is full*, *Not found on the server*…, and its **Details** show the error, on the main list and, with Swipe Between Sections, in Settings.
- **A synced song plays without Wi-Fi when pressed in the Play Queue or on an album, playlist or Favourites page already open**, on Q2 Pod firmware with `playlist`, from the card, instead of *Network Disconnected*. A song that isn't synced still says so (as does one a paused sync is about to replace).
- **Favourites syncs as its own playlist with Sync Entire Library on:** *Sync Favourites* and *Add to Sync* on its page add it, a **Favourites** playlist appears on the Q2 and follows your hearts (its songs, already in the library, are not downloaded twice), and it can be taken out again. Before, the Sync page said *In Sync* and no Favourites playlist ever appeared.
- **The Favourites row in Playlists is held to add it to Sync or take it out,** and shows *Waiting to sync* or *On Q2*, as the other playlists do.
- **Sign Out asks first**: the first press says *Press again to sign out*, as Clear Playback Cache does, since Sign Out stops a sync and the music and forgets plays and hearts not sent yet; any other press in between drops it. Afterwards the sign-in page no longer says *Signed in as* the user just signed out.
- **Clear Playback Cache's second press clears the cache:** the Quality and Storage page, which Q2 Pod asks again after the first press's message, no longer drops that first press.
- **A revoked or expired sign-in no longer says *Wrong username or password*** when no password was typed (the Q2 revoked under Dashboard → Devices, the server rebuilt, or a library the account lost): pages, the Sync page and Settings now say *Server refused this Q2. Sign in again: Settings → Account → Sign Out*. A wrong password typed at sign-in still says *Wrong username or password*.


## [0.7.0] - 2026-11-12

### Added

- **⋯ on Now Playing shows the Play Queue** while Jellyfin's songs play, on Q2 Pod firmware that reads `more=`: the songs queued, opening at the song playing (marked *Now Playing*, or *Started here* for the song you pressed until the Q2 says what plays), the songs after it following, then *Earlier songs*; press one to play the queue from it. The Q2's own ⋯ page had Playlists and Quality Choice, which mean nothing for them.
- **Plays are reported to your Jellyfin server**, which counts them as it does for its own apps (play count, *Played*, last played). A streamed song counts when it starts; a synced song played from the Q2's library counts the next time Jellyfin is open with Wi-Fi (and the Q2's clock is set), read from Q2 Pod's play log with the time it was played. Without Wi-Fi they wait on the card. **Settings → Report Plays to Server** turns it off. With a Q2 Pod that says which streamed song plays (`now`), every streamed song is reported as it plays, with how far into it you are and whether it's paused (the server's dashboard shows it), and when it stops (Jellyfin stays running while the queue plays); with an older Q2 Pod's `urls`, only the song you pressed in a list is counted.
- **The heart on Now Playing marks the song a favourite on your Jellyfin server** (and un-hearts it), for streamed songs and for synced songs played from the Q2's library, with a Q2 Pod that has hearts for card apps (`fav`). The heart changes at once; the change waits on the card until Jellyfin is open with Wi-Fi, and only Sign Out forgets it. One the server refuses is said once on Jellyfin's main page.

### Changed

- **The Streaming icon is the Jellyfin logo in its colours** (purple to blue) in the Modern and Classic themes; Minimal shows it grey, as its other Streaming icons.
- **Sign In with Password is one page** on Q2 Pod firmware with `field`: *Username:* (the name typed beneath), *Password:* (`***` once typed) and *Sign In*. A failure says why, with *Retry* (the page again, the password cleared: it is never sent twice) and *Cancel*. *Settings → Account → Signed in as* opens the same page. What was typed stays in the app's memory only, and is forgotten once signed in or on going back. Older firmware keeps the pages it had.
- **The server's address is a *Server Address:* row** on the first page and the Server page on Q2 Pod firmware with `field`: an address that doesn't work says why, with *Retry* and *Cancel*, instead of a page to press Return past.
- **No *Stop Jellyfin* row** on Jellyfin's Loading… and error pages on Q2 Pod firmware that reads `stop=0`: an error page has *Retry* and *Cancel*.
- **A sync takes a song already in the playback cache** at Sync Quality by moving it, instead of downloading it again, and Start Sync no longer clears from the cache the songs it is about to take.

### Fixed

- **Albums sorted Random opens on a large library:** it says *Loading albums…* while every album comes from the server, then shows them shuffled, instead of timing out each time Albums was opened.
- **A list that can't load still shows its Search and Sort** (Albums, Artists and Playlists, in any sort), so a sort that keeps failing can be changed there.

## [0.6.2] - 2026-11-12

### Changed

- **Playing a song, album or playlist opens Now Playing at once**: the black "Playing …" message shown first is gone. A song that has to download first still says "Buffering …".
- **Default Quality is now Sync Quality**, and it is every synced song's: the *Quality* rows on an album, artist, playlist, Favourites, a song and Entire Library are gone, and a quality saved for one item is no longer used. After a change of Sync Quality, the next sync brings the songs already on the Q2 again at the new quality.
- **Sync Quality and Streaming Quality list Original first, then Opus from 320 kbps down.** Streaming Quality no longer has *Same as Default Quality*: one left at it keeps the quality it had.
- **Settings → Account** shows the server, *Signed in as* and *Sign Out* (and, on a Q2 signed in with an API key, that an admin-level key is stored); what is stored on the card, and the tip to use a server user just for the Q2, are in the README.
- **Add to Sync answers at once** on an album's, artist's, playlist's or Favourites' page, and for a song on such a page: it uses the list the page was drawn from instead of asking the server for it again.
- **API key sign-in is turned off for now:** *Sign In with an API Key* is no longer offered, and a `Jellyfin sign-in.txt` with `apikey=` is deleted and refused with "API key sign-in is turned off for now". A Q2 already signed in with a key stays signed in.

### Fixed

- **Changing Sync Quality during a sync** starts that sync again at the new quality, so the songs it hadn't fetched yet no longer come at the old one. A paused sync stops instead and waits for *Start Sync* (holding an item in Sync Jobs while paused does the same), and choosing the quality already set changes nothing.
- **Changing Sync Quality after a finished sync** opens a new preview on the Sync page, with *Start Sync*, instead of the old *Sync complete* page.
- **A setting that can't be saved** (Sync Quality, Streaming Quality, Space to Keep Free, Playback Cache) says *Couldn't save the setting* instead of showing the new value.

## [0.6.1] - 2026-11-11

### Fixed

- **A typed password or API key is never a page's title, with or without Wi-Fi:** after signing in the page says *Login Success*; a wrong one says why. Q2 Pod titles a typed text's page with the text.
- **Retry on a wrong password or API key** no longer sends it to the server again (Jellyfin can lock out a user after failed sign-ins): it says why again; Return asks for another. A server that couldn't be reached is still tried again.
- **Retry on a page kept from before the app closed** sends nothing to the server: a password or key typed there is never searched for or shown; the page says *Press Return and search again*.
- **Return after signing in no longer says "No playlists called that"** or opens Sign In with Password again: the sign-in pages Return goes back through show Newly Added, Albums, Artists, Playlists and Settings, and the typed user name or password is never searched for.
- **Return from the user name's page to the server address's** shows the ways to sign in, not *User: https://…* (a password typed next was sent with the address as the user name).
- **Return after a server address that didn't work** goes back to the page it was typed on, however many were tried; the message says to press Return to try another.
- **Quick Connect says where to approve the code:** *User Menu → Quick Connect* (it said Settings).
- **Swiping back to Albums, Artists or Playlists** returns to the page and row you left, instead of the first page with a different row selected. Back to the main list (Swipe Between Sections off) still opens each list at its start.
- **A library update the Q2 never answers** no longer keeps the app running indefinitely: it may close two hours after asking.
- **Lyrics while streaming stop asking an unreachable server:** after two tries that can't reach it, the rest of the queue's lyrics wait for the next Play, instead of each song waiting up to 3 s in turn.
- **The clock from the server is checked against its certificate:** on a Q2 whose clock is unset, the time is now taken only when it falls within the server certificate's validity, and never before 2025, so an expired certificate can't set the clock back.
- **A search typed on Albums searches Albums** even after leaving an Artists or Playlists list that was still loading; before, the slow list could finish afterwards and make the search run there.
- **Sign Out wipes the stored sign-in** before deleting it, so it can't be recovered from the card.
- **Entire Library no longer removes songs** of an album the server's album list skipped while it changed during the preview.
- **A music library with more than one folder** keeps every folder's songs in playlists and syncs (only the first 200 top-level items were read).
- **The same song in two formats** gets its own lyrics file instead of sharing one.

### Changed

- **Sign Out** shows the sign-in once it has signed out.
- **Signed out, Change Server is first**, then Quick Connect, password and API key; it opens the Server page.
- **Settings → Account → Server:** the address is the row to press to type a new one, what that does beneath it.
- **Settings → Account → Signed in as** signs in as another user with a password; the sign-in now stays until the new one works.
- **Waiting for Quick Connect** shows only the *Code*, where to approve it and Cancel, which brings back the other ways to sign in.
- **The server's address** shows with its `https://`, and the box to type one says `https://` is added.
- **Settings is shorter:** On This Q2 and Sync Entire Library are now on the Sync page, whose options read Sync Jobs, On This Q2, Sync Favourites, Choose Playlists, Sync Entire Library, and stay at the top of the page whatever the sync is doing (running, paused, stopped, finished, or the server out of reach), with the sync's progress or result below them. Default Quality, Streaming Quality, Space to Keep Free, Playback Cache and Clear Playback Cache moved to a new **Quality and Storage** page, just below Sync in Settings.
- **Less battery and Wi-Fi use:** one cover download per album instead of per song row; lyrics kept between Plays and read from the card for synced songs, and no more tries once the server is out of reach; Play reuses the list just shown; Artists, Playlists and Newly Added reuse their list for a minute; fewer log lines and manifest writes; connections reused, then closed when idle; a failed cover waits a minute before another try; the app wakes every 5 s instead of every second when nothing is under way.
- **Settings' Connected line** now follows the app's last real request to the server (Not connected after one that failed or was refused) instead of pinging the server every 30 s.

### Added

- **Sorting Albums, Artists and Playlists**: a Sort page with *Sort By* and *Order*. Albums by Name, Release Date, Date Added, Album Artist or Random; Artists by Name, Date Added or Random; Playlists by Name, Date Added, Duration or Random (Favourites stays first). Each list keeps its choice. Random keeps its order while you scroll and come back; Order shuffles again. With Q2 Pod's `sort` cap the Sort page opens from an icon in the title bar, beside the search icon; otherwise from a *Sort* row at the top of the list.
- **Streaming through the player Tidal uses** (v0.6, with Q2 Pod 1.7.3's `urls` cap): a streamed song plays from the server as it arrives, with its cover in Now Playing, instead of being saved to the card first. On older firmware streaming works as before.
- **Covers in lists** (v0.6, with Q2 Pod 1.7.3's `art` cap): albums, artists and playlists show their cover beside each row.
- **Lyrics while streaming** (v0.6, with Q2 Pod's streamed-lyrics change, not yet in a release): Now Playing shows the server's lyrics for a streamed song, as for a synced one.
- **Search on Newly Added** (v0.6): a search of artists, albums and songs. With Q2 Pod 1.7.3, every section's search is an icon in the title bar.
- **Sync Jobs** (v0.6), on the Sync page and under a running sync: what is in Sync and not yet on the Q2. A press holds an item: it keeps its songs on the Q2 but adds no more until resumed, and a sync under way starts again without it, so the rest goes first.
- **The songs a sync adds** (v0.6): Sync's "+N songs" row opens their list.
- **Long lists a page at a time** (v0.6, with Q2 Pod 1.7.3's `window` cap): Artists, Albums, Playlists, search and an item's songs show 200 rows, then a *Next page* row; a later page starts with *Previous page*. Each loads in place, so Back leaves the list rather than paging back, and coming back from Now Playing reloads only that page. Later pages reuse the artist and playlist list for a minute instead of fetching it again.
- **Artists show their number of albums** (v0.6): it was always 0.
- First scaffold: a Jellyfin client (`internal/jellyfin`) for browsing, search, sync and streaming, with sign-in that keeps only an access token on the card, and Quick Connect support in the client.
- Sign-in on the Q2 itself, in order of preference: Quick Connect (no password reaches the Q2), user name and password typed on the Q2 (sent only to the server over HTTPS, never stored), or an admin-level API key with a warning. The server's address is typed on the Q2 too.
- **Quality**: Original (the server's file, no transcoding) by default, or an Opus transcode made by the server at 64, 96, 128, 160, 192, 224, 256, 288 or 320 kbps, chosen from a list for the default, for streaming and per item. Originals the Q2 can't play arrive as Opus 320. (Replaces q2-subsonic's MP3 320/128.)
- **Streaming starts before a song has all arrived** (v0.2; v0.4 below makes it more careful).
- **Lyrics from the Jellyfin server**, synced or plain, beside each synced or streamed song for Q2 Pod's Now Playing.
- **New layout**: Newly Added, Albums, Artists, Playlists and Settings; a search box at the top of Albums, Artists and Playlists for that section; Favourites at the top of Playlists; Sync, On This Q2 and Sync Entire Library under Settings.
- **Buffering feedback** (v0.5, replacing v0.4's buffering page): a *Buffering…* message, and the pressed song's row shows the progress until it plays; no extra page, so Return from Now Playing goes straight back to the list.
- **Next works on streamed songs again** (v0.5): the songs after the one playing start as early as their link allows (as the first does), so Q2 Pod queues them and Next can go to them; v0.4 waited for them to be whole, so Next did nothing until then.
- **Steadier streaming** (v0.4): only a FLAC or WAV original starts before it is whole, after 3 s on a connection at least twice the song's rate, otherwise after at least 15 s and enough to play through at 60 % of the measured speed (the slower of the whole download's and the last 3 seconds'). Transcodes and other formats play once whole, so their length and seeking are right.
- **Settings → Swipe Between Sections** (v0.4): on by default where the firmware offers sections; off on request; turned off when the card moves to firmware without them, and greyed out (unavailable) there.
- **Swipe between sections** (v0.3) on Q2 Pod firmware with the `sections` cap: each of the five sections is a page, and a sideways swipe changes page. Stream errors show at the top of Settings.
- **Sign-in**: after typing the server address, the sign-in choices and Change Server show at once.
- **Music only**: lists, search, favourites, playlists and sync are held to the server's Music libraries; films, audiobooks and mixed-content files never appear.
- **Sync Favourites** (one press adds your favourites and starts the sync) and **Choose Playlists** on the Sync page.
- **Space to Keep Free** (Settings, 512 MB by default): a sync stops before the card falls under it, says why, and keeps what it brought; Resume continues. A sync that doesn't all fit no longer refuses to start.
- Tested against a real Jellyfin 12.4.0 server: `contrib/testserver/` makes one in Docker with synthetic media.
- **Favourites**: your heart-marked Jellyfin songs, to play or to sync as a **Favourites** playlist.
- **Playback cache**, as Q2 Pod's Tidal cache: streamed songs stay on the card once played and replay without Wi-Fi. Size in Settings (Off to 8 GB, 2 GB by default), oldest played first out, **Clear Playback Cache**; it gives way to a sync that needs the space.
- Everything else from q2-subsonic: the Q2 Pod card-app integration, sync preview and engine, playlists, streaming, logs.
